# Changelog

## 2.0.1 — first public release

- One-click sequential Amex offer enrollment with progress and Stop.
- Detection for text and real-style plus-icon Add to Card buttons.
- Saved-offers scanning for manually saved or previously enrolled offers.
- Automatic saved-list scan and report after a completed enrollment run.
- Separate cash and Membership Rewards points analysis, expiry, planned purchases, and filters.
- CSV, standalone HTML, and print/PDF exports.
- Background readiness checks and clear update/reload messages.
- Limited permissions, local snapshots, and a clear-local-reports control.
- Documentation, fictional demos, and automated calculation/browser/integration checks.

This release covers English-language U.S. Amex pages and one selected card at a time.
